Armory

The Armory in Marauder's Sea is unique: troop weapons, then naval mines at level 4. Costs and upgrades.

Folklore

One shed per captain. That is the rule. The Armory is where fists become swords, then muskets, then cartridge guns — and where the sea mines are born.

In the game

One per player. Unique. Weapon tiers: swords, muskets, cartridge guns. Level 4 unlocks Naval Mines. Costs $500k / $1.5M / $3M then $2M. Upgradable. About 8 seconds to build.
